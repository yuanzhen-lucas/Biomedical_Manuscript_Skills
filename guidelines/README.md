# Reporting guideline registry

`registry.yaml` is JSON-compatible YAML and is loaded with Python's standard
library. It records the known edition, routing terms, authoritative discovery
URL, review date, and whether the edition needs a live currentness check.

Before applying an entry, check the linked official guideline source and the
target journal's current instructions. The registry is not a copy of checklist
text. Do not treat checklist completeness as study quality, risk of bias, or
statistical validity. For checklist reproduction, follow the source's copyright
and license terms.

Sources reviewed on 2026-10-30 included the [EQUATOR guideline library](https://www.equator-network.org/reporting-guidelines/), [STROBE-EPIC](https://equator-network.org/reporting-guidelines/strobe-epic/index.html), the [CONSORT/SPIRIT published statements](https://www.consort-spirit.org/published-statements), [PRISMA 2020](https://www.prisma-statement.org/prisma-2020), [PRISMA-S](https://www.prisma-statement.org/prisma-search), [PRISMA-C 2026](https://www.prisma-statement.org/children), [ARRIVE 2.0](https://arriveguidelines.org/arrive-guidelines), [CARE downloads](https://www.care-statement.org/downloads), [TRIPOD+AI](https://www.tripod-statement.org/), and the [TRIPOD-LLM statement](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41591-024-03425-5).
