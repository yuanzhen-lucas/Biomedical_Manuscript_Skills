# Research and publication ethics

This repository supports transparent reporting and does not replace ethics committees, IRBs/IACUCs, data protection officers, or institutional policy.

Author-supplied details must be verified for ethics approval, consent/assent, animal welfare, trial registration, funding, conflicts of interest, contributions, data availability, and AI use. Missing facts remain missing until confirmed by accountable researchers.

Authors remain responsible for AI-assisted material, citations, permission, accuracy, and any disclosure required by a journal. A reviewer or editor should not upload privileged peer-review text to an external AI service unless confidentiality is assured and applicable policy/permission allows it.

Check current [ICMJE Recommendations](https://www.icmje.org/recommendations/) and applicable [COPE guidance](https://publicationethics.org/) at the time of use. Journal and institutional rules may be more specific.
