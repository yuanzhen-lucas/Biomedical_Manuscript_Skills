# Reporting-guideline use

Reporting guidelines are design-specific recommendations for complete and transparent reporting. Choose the current statement and applicable extension from its official source and the journal's current instructions.

Do not use checklist completion to infer sound methods, low risk of bias, valid statistics, or correct conclusions. Keep four outputs distinct: reporting audit, methodological/risk-of-bias assessment, statistical audit, and claim-evidence audit.

The dated routing registry is `guidelines/registry.yaml`. It does not bundle checklist wording. Check upstream terms and licenses before downloading or reproducing checklist content.
