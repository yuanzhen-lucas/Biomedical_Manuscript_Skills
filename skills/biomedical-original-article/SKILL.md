# Original research workflow

## When to use
Use for IMRaD planning, evidence-grounded drafting, or consistency review of original human, animal, laboratory, epidemiological, diagnostic, prognostic, or omics research.

## When not to use
Do not use to create results, methods, ethics approvals, registrations, or data that were not supplied and verified.

## Required inputs
Study design, actual methods, validated results/data outputs, research question, and manuscript text or drafting request.

## Optional inputs
Protocol/SAP, tables/figures, ethics and consent details, funding/COI, registrations, dataset/code, and journal target.

## Workflow
1. Route study design to the reporting-guideline skill.
2. Draft sections only from author-provided study facts and traceable evidence.
3. Separate Methods from Results and Results from Discussion.
4. Flag missing denominators, methods, prespecification, ethics, data availability, and disclosures.
5. Compare abstract, text, tables, figures, and supplements; run claim and statistical triage.

## Dependencies
Reporting-guideline, statistical-review, claim-audit, and scientific-editing skills.

## Scientific safeguards
Keep missing values explicit. Do not silently strengthen causal claims or translate preclinical results to human efficacy.

## Failure behavior
Use `[DATA REQUIRED]`, `[METHOD REQUIRED]`, or `[AUTHOR CONFIRMATION REQUIRED]` when facts are absent.

## Output contract
Deliver section-specific draft or audit findings with source/data provenance, uncertainties, and required author inputs.

## Verification steps
Reconcile all numbers and statements against source data/outputs and protocol; verify all ethics and disclosure statements with authors.

## Human-review requirements
Study authors approve methods/results, interpretations, authorship, ethics statements, and every submitted version.
