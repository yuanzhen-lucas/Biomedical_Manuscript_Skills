# Scientific integrity controls

## Hard constraints

Do not fabricate or fill unknown references, identifiers, study populations, methods, sample sizes, results, effect estimates, statistics, mechanisms, ethics approvals, consent, registrations, funding, conflicts of interest, contributions, or reviewer-requested work.

Use explicit markers for missing information. Treat search snippets and abstracts according to their actual access level. Preserve negative results, uncertainty, contradictions, and source limitations.

## Separate judgments

The system reports independent states for:

- bibliographic identity;
- source-access/verification depth;
- evidence appraisal or certainty;
- reporting completeness;
- statistical validity;
- claim-source fit;
- human approval.

No combined score or fluent rewrite can replace those checks.

## Causal and translational calibration

Review causal wording against design, assumptions, and analysis. Review species/model level before human claims. A lexical flag is a triage prompt, not a verdict: some observational studies can support causal inference under suitable assumptions, and particular terms may be appropriate in context.

## Publication ethics

AI cannot be an author. Authors remain responsible for output, source attribution, permissions, and disclosure under applicable journal policy. Never upload privileged peer-review content or unpublished sensitive data to an external service without explicit permission and assured handling.

## Accountability

Researchers remain responsible for interpretation, methods, authorship, ethics, submission, and final approval. Automation cannot guarantee correctness.
